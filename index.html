[1] Hook

100 library branches. One in every neighbourhood?

Toronto has 158 neighbourhoods. Many of them have no physical library branch inside their boundaries.

Residents may still use branches in nearby neighbourhoods. This shows location, not travel access.

[2] Rising insight 1

Where does the budget go?

73.3%

CAD 7.158B of the CAD 9.766B selected total, 2025 approved gross operating budget

Four of our eight selected services - TTC conventional transit, Children's Services, Toronto Police Service, and Toronto Employment & Social Services - make up CAD 7.158B, or 73.3%, of the CAD 9.766B selected total.

Approved 2025 operating allocations, not actual spending. Share is of 8 selected services, not the whole city budget.

The rate does not rise steadily with income; the gap is mainly between the higher group and the other two.

[5] Main message

Look beneath the headline

Before judging whether a community is well served, residents should ask what a number includes and how many people it serves.

Headline totals give an incomplete picture of Toronto's public resources.

They cannot show how resources compare across services and communities.

[6] Solution

How can residents make better comparisons?

Specific funding or siting decisions need more evidence; the first step is asking better questions of the data.

Limitations

  • Approved budgets are not actual spending.
  • Mixed-year snapshot.
  • Associations, not causes.
  • Neighbourhood boundaries are not library service areas.
